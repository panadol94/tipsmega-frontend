## Main Findings
1. Core SEO/article pages are fast on desktop.
2. Mobile performance is the real bottleneck now.
3. `/trusted` is the weakest page and likely drags due to heavy client-side rendering, decorative animation, and media-rich cards.
4. Homepage mobile also has JS/main-thread overhead even though desktop is strong.
